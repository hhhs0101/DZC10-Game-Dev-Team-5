# Third-Party Asset Notices

## Fruit and Veggies Asset Pack — Helm3t

Vegetable enemy artwork used locally in this project is by **Helm3t** (the included pack identifies the author as Helmet).

- [Official asset page and download](https://helm3t.itch.io/free-32x32-fruits-and-veggies-asset-pack)
- [Creator profile](https://helm3t.itch.io/)
- Local edition: 32x32 Fruit and Veggie Pack, Free full edition (legacy).
- Local terms: `Licence and details.txt` included in the downloaded pack.
- Terms checked: October 20, 2026.

The supplied terms permit commercial and noncommercial game use and editing for projects. They prohibit redistributing the original or modified assets and restrict use in logos/trademarks, game-making tools, code templates, AI, NFT and cryptocurrency projects. Consult the original terms for the complete conditions.

The asset page also displays a CC BY-ND 4.0 metadata label alongside custom terms that prohibit asset redistribution. This repository does not rely on that label to redistribute artwork.

The source PNGs, sprite sheets, original archive and imported pack contents are **not included in Git**. Locally imported artwork and its supplied license remain under the ignored `Assets/Art/ThirdParty/Helm3tsVeggies/` directory. Tracked enemy definitions, code and Unity prefabs do not grant rights to the author's artwork.

To restore the vegetable artwork after cloning:

1. Obtain the pack separately from the official page and review its current terms.
2. Extract the downloaded pack.
3. In Unity, choose **Defense > Enemies > Import Veggies from Folder...** and select `VEGGIES/VEGGIES_NL`.
4. The importer copies the required images into the ignored local directory and reconnects the existing enemy definitions.

Missing local artwork uses the existing placeholder presentation. Do not force-add or copy the pack artwork into tracked directories without authorization from its creator.
